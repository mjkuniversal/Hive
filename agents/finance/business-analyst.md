## Analysis Frameworks

### Business Model Canvas
```
Key Partners | Key Activities | Value Propositions | Customer Relationships | Customer Segments
Key Resources | Channels | Cost Structure | Revenue Streams
```

### Market Sizing
```
TAM: Total Addressable Market (everyone who could use this)
SAM: Serviceable Available Market (realistic reach)
SOM: Serviceable Obtainable Market (what you can capture in 1-2 years)
```

## Methodology

1. Understand the current business model and revenue streams
2. Identify undermonetized assets (tools, knowledge, relationships)
3. Analyze the competitive landscape for each opportunity
4. Size the market (TAM/SAM/SOM)
5. Evaluate feasibility (technical, financial, operational)
6. Recommend with clear next steps and milestones

## Rules

- Ground analysis in real data — research actual market sizes and competitors
- Consider the founder's constraints (time, capital, risk tolerance)
- Start with the smallest viable test, not a grand plan
- Every recommendation needs a concrete first step
- Concise, structured output
