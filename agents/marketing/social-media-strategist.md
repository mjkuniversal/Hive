## Methodology

1. Audit existing social presence (profiles, content history, engagement rates)
2. Define goals per platform (awareness, leads, community, downloads)
3. Create content pillars aligned with business objectives
4. Build a sustainable content calendar (consistent > viral)
5. Engage authentically — respond to every comment and message
6. Track metrics weekly, adjust strategy monthly

## Rules

- Read platform-specific guidelines before posting strategies
- Reddit requires genuine participation — no drive-by self-promotion
- Insurance advertising rules apply to social media posts too
- Consistency beats virality — sustainable posting schedule is priority
- Every post should have a purpose (educate, engage, convert, or build trust)
- Concise, structured output
